# Directory homepage: space and task hierarchy

Reviewed November 22, 2026. This is a comparison of documented product patterns, not a claim that the sites share Pioneer Clubs' audience or constraints.

| Product | Relevant pattern | Pioneer Clubs decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a518701) | The home navigation provides a persistent account entry and search. | Keep the signed-in account and My space in the navigation; do not repeat a welcome banner above the page's purpose. |
| [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/authentication/keeping-your-account-and-data-secure/switching-between-accounts) | Account identity and account actions live in the profile menu. | Use the existing avatar/menu to show who is signed in. |
| [Notion](https://www.notion.com/help/navigate-with-the-sidebar) | Home, search, and personal content have stable entry points. | Keep My space reachable in navigation while the directory remains about discovering clubs. |
| [Figma Community](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038510693-Guide-to-the-Figma-Community) | Browse categories and search are prominent discovery tools. | Bring club search and filters closer to the top of the page. |
| [Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/479) | Browsing starts with a clear search; filters appear when needed. [Saved listings](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1236) are available as a separate destination. | Keep filters collapsed and show a saved-club shortcut only when there are saved clubs. |

The previous signed-in page repeated membership in the account control, a full-width welcome strip, and a shortlist strip even when the count was zero. The welcome strip had no unique task. Removing it and shortening the hero gives the directory more room without hiding sign-in status or saved clubs. The school review notice remains visible because it communicates publication status to every visitor.

Check at narrow and wide widths that the account menu or mobile drawer still exposes My space, the hero text and image do not overflow, the first search control stays reachable, and an active shortlist is linked from the directory heading. The page's hero copy remains editable through the existing Puck Directory Experience fields.
